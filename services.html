V1S04_FAQ / Before you ask
How much does a shoot cost?

Every job is quoted to scope: hours on site, number of cuts and how fast you need it. Send the details and you'll get a clear quote within a day or two. (Placeholder — Lewis to confirm starting rates.)

How fast is turnaround?

Event recaps land in 48 to 72 hours. Brand and campaign films usually take one to two weeks depending on revisions.

Do you travel?

Based in Edmonton, regularly in Calgary and Toronto, and happy to go wherever the project is. Travel is quoted at cost.

Do you shoot photos too?

Yes. Video first, photo second. Most packages include a set of photo selects from the same shoot.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included on every edit. More can be added if the project needs it. (Placeholder — Lewis to confirm.)
